Frequently asked questions

Last updated: November 17, 2026

What is Campus Tech?
Campus Tech @ University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign is a student organization that builds products for campus life. It was registered as a Registered Student Organization (RSO) at UIUC in November 2026.
What does Campus Tech build?
AI Chat (iguide.chat/chat) for questions about UIUC, Dorms (iguide.chat/dorms) for comparing UIUC residence halls, a 3D room scan, apartment comparison, websites for registered student organizations, and tools for local businesses.
Who leads Campus Tech?
Michael Li (President), George Wang (Tech Lead), Lai Wei (Business Director), Nancy Zhang (Membership Director) and Chris Lopez (Treasurer), with Skylar Chu as advisor.
How do I join Campus Tech?
Submit the membership application and complete a short interview. Fall 2026 Round 1 applications close at 11:59 PM Central Time on Sunday, November 29, 2026.
Which roles are open?
Programming, design, marketing, business and administration.
Do I need coding experience to join?
No. Only programming roles involve code.
Who can join?
Any UIUC student, of any major and year. Domestic and international students are both welcome.
Can I suggest a product idea?
Yes. Anyone can pitch an idea through the Pitch Your Idea form; no full plan or coding is needed.
How do I contact Campus Tech?
Email techadmin.campustech@gmail.com.
Is this an official University of Illinois website?
No. Campus Tech is a registered student organization, and this is not an official University website.
