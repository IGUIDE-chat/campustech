Campus Tech Fall 2026 Round 1 Recruiting: Roles and How to Apply

Published November 17, 2026 · Campus Tech at UIUC

Campus Tech at UIUC is recruiting new members for Fall 2026 Round 1. Students of any major and year can apply, and both domestic and international students are welcome.

Open roles

  • Programming: build our products, from websites to AI features.
  • Design: design product interfaces and visuals.
  • Marketing: content and social media, including Xiaohongshu.
  • Business: partnerships and sponsorship.
  • Administration: operations that keep the organization running.

How to apply

  1. Fill in the membership application. It takes about 5 minutes.
  2. We will reach out by email to schedule a short interview.

Deadline

Round 1 applications close at 11:59 PM Central Time on Sunday, November 29, 2026, the last day of Fall Break. Recruiting happens in several rounds, so if you miss this one, watch for the next.

Frequently asked questions

Who can join Campus Tech?
Any student at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, of any major and year. Domestic and international students are both welcome.
Do I need coding experience?
No. Only programming roles involve code; design, marketing, business and administration roles do not.
How does joining work?
Every member joins through the application and a short interview.
When is the Fall 2026 Round 1 deadline?
11:59 PM Central Time on Sunday, November 29, 2026.
Can I join as an advisor?
Yes. Faculty and staff interested in advising can get in touch through the same application form or by emailing techadmin.campustech@gmail.com.
