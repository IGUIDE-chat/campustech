Have an Idea for a Campus Product? Pitch It to Campus Tech

Published November 17, 2026 · Campus Tech @ UIUC

Have an idea for a product that would make campus life better? You can pitch it to Campus Tech @ UIUC, even if you are not a member.

What to tell us

  • Who would use it: which students or groups on campus.
  • What problem it solves: what is hard, slow or missing today.
  • What skills it needs: what you would need from other people to build it.

You do not need a full plan, and you do not need to know how to code.

How to pitch

Use the Pitch Your Idea form. Our team reads every idea, and we will reach out by email if we want to talk.

What happens next

Promising ideas can become Campus Tech projects, built by members across programming, design, marketing and business. If you would like to help build it yourself, also apply to join.
