Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This policy explains what information the Campus Tech website (campustech.iguide.chat) handles. Campus Tech @ University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign ("Campus Tech", "we") is a registered student organization at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign.

What this website collects

This is a static website. It has no accounts, no sign-up, no cookies and no analytics or advertising trackers. We do not sell or share personal information.

  • Theme preference. If you switch between dark and light mode, your choice is stored in your browser's local storage on your own device so the site remembers it. It is never sent to us. You can clear it at any time in your browser settings.
  • Server logs. Like any website, the hosting provider that serves these pages may keep standard technical logs (for example IP address, browser type and the page requested) for security and reliability. We do not use these logs to identify visitors.

Third-party services

  • Google Fonts. The page font is loaded from Google, which receives your IP address when the font is requested. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Google Forms. Our membership application and idea submission forms are hosted on Google Forms. What you enter there is collected by Google and shared with Campus Tech officers. We use it only to review applications and ideas and to contact you, and we do not sell it.
  • Links to other sites. Links such as iguide.chat and GitHub lead to sites with their own privacy policies.

Form submissions

Application and idea submissions are accessible only to current Campus Tech officers who need them. We keep them for as long as they are useful for recruiting or following up on an idea, and you can ask us to delete your submission at any time.

Children

This website is meant for university students and is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

To ask what we hold about you, or to have a form submission corrected or deleted, email techadmin.campustech@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
